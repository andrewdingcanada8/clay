## Where it stands
- Full bake (harness prepareMs): ~-15..-30 % (two-level coarse, copy pass); the rest is map() near the surface:
  the coarse band (band tiles ~21 % composite, 38 % sweeps-64) and the brick samples (2.2M composite, 7.7M sweeps-64)
  at 1.5-2.7 µs each, plus ~0.33 µs per fragment of the big shader's setup even where a branch skips map()
  (so any "cheap branch" inside the FRAG shader saves less than it looks).
- Incremental (an edit's real time to sharp): 5-9x the full bake where the change is local; a change that covers the
  scene (moving the main body) is a full bake.
- Paced (app frame loop) incremental composite: ~2.8 s wall for ~1.1 s of GPU work (one fenced batch per frame).
- Next: fewer brick samples (adaptive N, or THR), a 48³ level under the low field, a progressive order (bricks on
  screen first), more batches per frame for the cheap passes.
